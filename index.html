About Luna

A small store, put together slowly.

Luna started as a course project and turned into a place for the pieces I keep coming back to: one good bag, a perfume that lasts the day, headphones that actually block the street.

No endless catalogue, no drop-shipping. Nine products, photographed one by one, with honest prices and a checkout that takes under a minute.
